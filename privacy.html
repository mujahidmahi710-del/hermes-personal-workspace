Privacy policy

Effective October 19, 2026 · Hermes Personal Workspace

This policy describes this privately operated personal Google Workspace integration. It is separate from the policies of Google, the hosting provider, Nous Research, and any model or service provider the owner configures.

1. Information accessed

With account authorization, the integration can access Gmail messages and metadata; Drive files and metadata; Calendar information; and Sheets and Docs content. Access is limited by granted Google permissions and the tasks the owner authorizes. Contacts access is not requested by this installation.

2. Purpose of access

Google user data is used to perform requested personal-assistant tasks, such as locating files, managing authorized email or calendar tasks, and working with documents and spreadsheets. Broad API permission does not itself authorize every possible action.

3. Processing and sharing

The assistant runs from the owner's controlled installation. Relevant task content may be sent to the owner's configured AI model provider for processing or to explicitly enabled services and recipients when needed for an authorized task. This is not a promise that all task processing happens offline. Provider-specific retention and processing terms also apply.

The integration does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models. Any service used to process Google user data must be configured and used consistently with applicable Google API data-use requirements.

4. Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored separately from this public website in the owner's local installation. Task content may remain in local conversations, task records, logs, or backups until the owner deletes it. Content deliberately saved or sent to Google or another authorized destination remains subject to that destination's retention and deletion controls.

5. Control and deletion

Account holders can revoke access through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents further API access but does not automatically delete previously retained local records or copies already sent to authorized destinations. The owner can remove local credentials, retained records, and backups, and request deletion from relevant service providers where applicable.

6. Website visitors

This static information site includes no application-added analytics, advertising, forms, tracking scripts, or embedded account content. Its hosting provider may process standard request logs and other technical information under its own privacy policy. Visiting this site does not authorize the integration.

7. Security and limitations

Credentials and private account content must not be committed to the website's source repository or deployed with its public files. Access controls and device security are the installation owner's responsibility. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Google API Limited Use

Hermes Personal Workspace's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

9. Contact and changes

For questions, access removal, or deletion requests, authorized users should contact the installation owner through their existing private communication channel. Material changes to this policy will be reflected here with an updated effective date.

A privately operated personal integration. Not an official Google or Nous Research service.
