Hermes Personal Workspace connects its owner's assistant to selected Google services, so personal tasks can be handled through authorized API access.

This is an information site. It has no sign-in form, collects no Google credentials, and provides no access to the owner's accounts.

What the integration does

Depending on the owner's instructions and granted permissions, the local assistant can work with Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Google Sheets, and Google Docs.

Examples include finding a file, reviewing a calendar, preparing an email, and updating a document. Actions remain subject to the owner's instructions and approval requirements.

Access belongs to the owner

Each Google account is connected separately through Google's OAuth consent process. This personal installation is not a public registration service. Publicly viewing this site does not grant access to any connected account.

Privacy, in plain language

Google credentials and Workspace content are not served by this website. The integration's handling of account permissions, task data, local records, and connected processing services is explained in the privacy policy.

Support

This installation is managed by its owner. Authorized users can contact the owner through their existing private communication channel for support, access removal, or data-deletion requests.

A privately operated personal integration. Not an official Google or Nous Research service.
